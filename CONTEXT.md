# maxpattillo.com

The personal site of Max Pattillo, published at maxpattillo.com. It began as a fork of Bryce DeCora's site and is now maintained independently.

## Language

**Owner**:
Max Pattillo, the person the site is about and the subject of its Person entity.
_Avoid_: author, user, Bryce

**CloseBot**:
The company where the Owner works as its first employee and a developer. It is the Owner's employer, not the site's subject.
_Avoid_: the company, my company

**Origin project**:
Bryce DeCora's site (`closebotai/brycedecora`), the code this site was forked from. It is credited but no longer tracked.
_Avoid_: upstream, template, parent

**Article**:
A piece of long-form writing published under `/writing/`.
_Avoid_: post, blog post, entry
